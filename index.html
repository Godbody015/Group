About the Company

About Infin Group

Infin Group is an emerging service provider specialising in security, cleaning and construction services. Our goal is to deliver reliable, professional and efficient solutions that meet the needs of businesses, organisations and individuals.

We are committed to maintaining high standards of service, employing competent personnel and building lasting relationships with our clients through professionalism, integrity and attention to detail.

As a growing company, we aim to establish a strong presence in Gauteng and expand our services through quality workmanship, customer satisfaction and consistent service delivery.

Our services are designed to work together. A site that is secure, clean and well maintained is easier to run, safer for the people who use it and better for the people who own it. By offering security, cleaning and construction under one roof, Infin Group gives clients a single, dependable point of contact rather than several suppliers to manage.

We serve offices and commercial premises, residential clients, shared facilities, events and building projects. Every engagement begins with understanding the client’s needs, and we develop a tailored approach that fits the site, the schedule and the budget. Regular quality checks and ongoing training keep our people and our standards consistent from the first day to the last.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver dependable security and cleaning services that meet our clients’ needs, protect people and property, and promote a clean, safe and welcoming environment. We are committed to maintaining professional standards and continuously improving the quality of our services.

Our Vision

To be recognised as the leading provider of integrated security and cleaning solutions, setting the benchmark for quality, innovation and customer satisfaction in the industry.
